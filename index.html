      How these numbers are worked out
      • Grid: 0.727 kg of CO₂ per kWh, India's weighted average for 2023-24 reported by the Central Electricity Authority.
      • Laptop 35 W and desktop with screen 100 W, inside the usual 20-50 W and 60-200 W ranges. Shutting down for 6 hours saves that much.
      • Screen: one modern 24-inch ENERGY STAR monitor draws about 12.5 W on and 0.12 W asleep. We count one hour at lunch.
      • Morning light: an 18 W LED fitting kept off for 3 hours. This is our assumption, so change it to match your office.
      • Paper: about 18.6 g of CO₂ per A4 sheet. Double-sided on a 10-page print saves 5 sheets.
      • Cups, water and meal packs are example amounts. These are estimates, not meter readings.

      Where the facts come from

      • U.S. Department of Energy: computers and office equipment for sleep and shutdown timing, power strips, standby and screen savers.
      • CEA CO₂ baseline database report for the India grid factor.
      • Bijli Bachao: laptop and desktop energy use for typical watts.
      • ENERGY STAR certified display listing for monitor on and sleep power.
      • Print impact formula for CO₂ per sheet of paper.
      • Government of India reply in Parliament for AC settings: about 6% saved per 1°C, and the 24-26°C range.
      • U.S. Department of Energy: LED lighting for LED energy use.
      • Wikipedia: standby power for the share of household use.
